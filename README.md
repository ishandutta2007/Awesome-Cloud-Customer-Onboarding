## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects

*Sorted by GitHub Stars Count (Descending)* 🌟

- **[Novu](https://github.com/novuhq/novu)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/novuhq/novu?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/novuhq/novu/stargazers)  
  **Open-source notification infrastructure for customer onboarding**, MIT licensed. **36,000+ stars**. **Multi-channel workflow engine** for onboarding emails, in-app bell notifications, SMS, and webhooks. **Self-hosted Docker & Helm deployments** for privacy-conscious applications. 🔔

- **[Chatwoot](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/chatwoot/chatwoot?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot/stargazers)  
  **Open-source omnichannel customer support desk (Intercom/Zendesk alternative)**, MIT licensed. **25,000+ stars**. **Built-in dashboard** showing response times, custom account attributes, and conversation context. **The communication backbone for customer onboarding**. 💬

- **[PostHog](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/PostHog/posthog?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog/stargazers)  
  **Open-source product analytics & user onboarding funnels**, MIT licensed. **23,000+ stars**. Track onboarding conversion drop-offs, user session replays, and feature flag rollouts during user setup. 📈

- **[Formbricks](https://github.com/formbricks/formbricks)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/formbricks/formbricks?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/formbricks/formbricks/stargazers)  
  **Open-source experience management & onboarding survey platform**, AGPL licensed. **8,500+ stars**. Targeted in-app micro-surveys and CSAT/NPS feedback during customer onboarding. 📋

- **[Laudspeaker](https://github.com/laudspeaker/laudspeaker)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/laudspeaker/laudspeaker?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/laudspeaker/laudspeaker/stargazers)  
  **Open-source customer engagement & product onboarding platform**, open-source. **2,216+ stars, 153 forks**. **The open-source alternative to Customer.io, Appcues, and Braze**. Design product onboarding flows and event-triggered email/SMS campaigns. 🚀

- **[Usertour](https://github.com/usertour/usertour)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/usertour/usertour?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/usertour/usertour/stargazers)  
  **Open-source user onboarding platform**, open-source. **1,444 stars, 79 forks**. **The open-source alternative to Userflow and Appcues**. Create in-app product tours, checklists, and surveys with complete data ownership. 🎨

- **[CXM-Tool](https://github.com/antigravitysoham-eng/CXM-Tool)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/antigravitysoham-eng/CXM-Tool?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/antigravitysoham-eng/CXM-Tool/stargazers)

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️
- **Open-source customer onboarding tools are not free** — Novu, Chatwoot, Laudspeaker, Usertour, and Atrium are excellent with zero license cost, but their real cost is **maintenance**. Someone runs upgrades, wires integrations, configures email delivery, and answers questions when a portal breaks.
- Open-source solutions provide self-hosted ownership and data sovereignty, but enterprise-grade SLA guarantees and 24/7 support remain primarily commercial offerings. **Always validate onboarding flows with real customer data** before rolling out at scale. 🚀
